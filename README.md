# India-Consumer-Price-Index-Inflation-Analysis
End-to-end data analysis of India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation trends (2017–2023), covering category weights, COVID-19 impacts, and global crude oil correlations.

**India Consumer Price Index (CPI) Inflation Analysis:**

Comprehensive data analysis and deep dive into India's retail inflation trends, examining category contributions, post-pandemic impacts, and macroeconomic drivers (2017–2023).

**📌 Executive Summary of Findings**

**Q1: CPI Basket Category Contributions (Latest Month: May 2023)**
Key Finding: Based on the latest available monthly data for May 2023, Pan, Tobacco & Intoxicants recorded the highest relative index contribution of 18.3% towards the overall CPI calculation framework.

Methodology: Evaluated underlying index values across broader bucket combinations to determine relative category weights summing to 100%.

**Q2: Year-over-Year (Y-o-Y) Inflation Trends (2017–2023)**
Peak Inflation Year: 2022 recorded the highest overall average annual inflation level across the 2017–2022 timeframe, while 2021 registered the highest annual growth rate jump at 21% (excluding partial 2023 data).
Core Drivers (2022 Peak):
Food & Beverages Surge: Hit a cumulative index high of 24,836, driven by global supply chain bottlenecks, rising fertilizer expenses, and erratic weather (heatwaves and unseasonal rains).
Fuel and Light Shock: Crossed the 2,102 mark (up from 1,909 in 2021) due to geopolitical tensions and the Russia-Ukraine conflict, causing international energy costs (crude oil, natural gas, coal) to skyrocket.
Broad-Based Cost of Living Spikes: Universal increases across Clothing ($2,114$), Housing ($2,017$), and Miscellaneous Services ($12,106$) as post-pandemic demand rapidly outpaced supply.

**Q3: Broader Food Bucket Trends (12 Months Ending May 2023)**
Month-on-Month (MoM) Food Inflation Extremes:
Highest Inflation Month: October 2022 ($+1.01\%$)
Lowest Inflation Month: December 2022 ($-1.35\%$)
Biggest Individual Sub-Category Driver: Spices emerged as the primary driver within the food bucket, recording the highest absolute percentage change of 16.5% over the 12-month period.

**Q4: Impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic (Pre vs. Post March 2020)**
Comparison Window: Pre-COVID (Mar'19–Feb'20) vs. Post-COVID (Mar'20–Feb'21)
Food & Beverages: Average monthly inflation decelerated from 0.9% (Pre) down to 0.4% (Post) as supply chains and agricultural logistics stabilized.
Transport & Communication: Experienced a sharp escalation, doubling from 0.5% to 0.9% due to fuel price volatility and pandemic-induced movement restrictions.
Housing & Fuel/Light: Remained relatively stable or saw minor downward adjustments (Housing: 0.4% to 0.3%; Fuel & Light: 0.5% to 0.3%).
Health: Maintained steady upward pressure, ticking up slightly from 0.4% to 0.5% amidst public health demands.

**Q5: Influence of Global Crude Oil Price Fluctuations (2021–2023)**
Methodology: Month-on-month percentage changes in imported crude oil prices (USD/Barrel, sourced via EIA Historical Data) were compared against CPI category inflation changes using the =CORREL statistical function.
Core Insights:
Weak Short-Term Correlation: Most consumer categories exhibited low correlation coefficients (between $-10\%$ and $+14\%$) due to domestic taxation, managed retail price caps, and price-smoothing mechanisms deployed by marketing companies in India.
Top Correlated Categories: Pan, tobacco & intoxicants (13.9%), Fuel and light (13.5%), and Clothing & footwear (13.0%) showed the highest relative positive sensitivity to imported energy and material shifts.
